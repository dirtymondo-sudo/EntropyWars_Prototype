# Current creative direction

## User requirements

Disaster City is a modern city in an adventure RPG with Zelda-like exploration and story-driven tactical JRPG combat. Exploration can become battle at any moment. Every character should be recruitable and have useful combat abilities, including ordinary citizens with abstract roles.

The city should feel like a dungeon, labyrinth or maze through thoughtful routes, loops, gates, secrets and shortcuts. Drivable roads, sidewalks, alleys, interiors and walkable rooftops all matter. Retain understandable orientation; this is not a request for random confusing corridors.

Plots are 12×12 tactical cells. Each cell is about 3–4 meters, with finer detail placement. The current layout chooses 4 meters. Buildings remain within one plot, but plots should have varied building sizes and counts, up to two or three buildings. Multi-floor buildings need accessible vertical circulation. No exact height limit is established. PvP compatibility is secondary.

Every building needs everyday, RPG and tactical functions.

The city has a future cyberpunk version with the same recognizable geography and buildings. Push future design toward an extreme 2047-to-year-3000 cyberpunk / retro-futurist mix, providing a strong contrast with dinosaurs. A time-machine glitch causes dinosaurs to overrun the future city; robots and kaiju cooperate against them. Dinosaurs are not modern-city residents.

The opening Bayside quest is inspired by alleged shadow-being sightings at a Miami mall. The game fictionalizes the premise. Multiple blocks of police vehicles and helicopters are important for cinematics. Shadow beings should be approximately 10 feet tall, INSIDE the mall, not giant outdoor figures.

The preferred art is realistic-proportioned, PS1/early-PS2 low-poly graphics: coarse textures, chunky silhouettes, restrained lighting, fog, nostalgic cult-classic atmosphere, esoteric clues, deadpan satire, conspiratorial urban adventure. The latest images were praised. Avoid returning to hyper-detailed panoramic concept painting.

The Woods already contains the haunted house and graveyard. Include a normal city church for Priest and Nun. Halfdemons are wealthy, charming, attractive male and female socialites in their mid-30s with Illuminati-like connections and possible antichrist implications. Vampire stays outside Disaster City. Atlantis/deep-ocean content remains separate from Bayside.

## Current proposed district organization

Residential / Southside; Civic Center; Financial; University; Downtown / Hero Plaza; Entertainment; Industrial; Sports / Culture; Bayside.

Current layout: 12×12 plots, 576×576 meters; each district has 16 plots. It includes 23 open plots, 45 single-building plots, 57 two-building plots and 19 three-building plots. There are 216 buildings, 210 ground connections and 33 selected roof crossings.

The latest layout was positively received, but numerical values and individual assignments remain editable design decisions.

## Current proposed opening route

Arrival P08-10 → market P08-09 → food hall P09-09 → security P10-09 → indoor mall concourse P10-10. A service pass represents the initial entry condition. After the encounter, P09-10 becomes the short return. The east-shop shutter offers another exit.

The pass and specific sequence are proposals, not finalized dialogue or quest lore. Police staging is P08-11; medical staging is P10-11.
