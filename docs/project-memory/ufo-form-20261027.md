mondo asked on 10-27 for two things. First, the grey and any race with the ufo family should turn into the UFO like the Time Cop does, with custom spin animations like the Seraphim and Occulus. Second, the Time Cop should be the future cop by default. PR #601 merged; token 20261027-ufoform-01-cors; zip r2-sync/ENTROPY_WARS_SYNC_20261027-0813.zip.

- battle.js wires every RACE_FAMILIES race with 'ufo' (the grey and the martian) after UNIT_ANIM_OVERRIDES. A ufo-family cast sets the UFO look (the race sprite url + '#ufo'). Any other cast sets the base url back. The look persists like the Time Cop's.
- sprites.js has UFO_FORM_3D (Assets/misc Triangle UFO, heightRatio 0.6) and wireUfoForm. three-renderer.js has procRig 'ufoCraft' with _UFO_POSES, a tractor beam cone and a hidden floor point for the hover.
- Shared renderer fixes:
  - _castRig replays the cast clip on a rig that was swapped in mid-cast.
  - rig.shotEntry carries _ew_oneShot across a structural rebuild. Before, a rebuild mid-cast cut the cast to idle at about 350 ms, on any unit.
- Time Cop: the base def is the cyberpunk cop. Its 2D sprite and portrait are still the police officer png, because there is no future-cop art.
- Calls flagged to mondo:
  - The look persists until the next cast, and a non-UFO cast reverts it.
  - Adaptable kits that borrow a ufo spell don't transform.
  - The beam is mostly hidden under the hull from the high board camera.
- Shots: /mnt/project-files/ufo-form/. Harness tip: ranked online has a 60 s shot clock, so chain the cast and the shots in one call. ThreeRenderer.devUnitAnim(uid) is a read-only clip probe.

- PR #611 (10-27, token 20261027-ufocraft-01-cors, zip ENTROPY_WARS_SYNC_20261027-0911.zip): a craft for each alien. sprites.js UFO_CRAFT_BY_RACE: bligblog flies the existing Assets/misc spaceship (procRig shipCraft: nose turned, spins as barrel rolls). The martian stays on the triangle.
- PR #614 (token 20261027-saucer-01-cors, zip ENTROPY_WARS_SYNC_20261027-0931.zip): the grey flies mondo's saucer, Assets/misc/UFO_Low_Poly.fbx (procRig saucerCraft). _ufoBuild scales any hull to the triangle's size. _ufoSwatchSheets turns a sheetless file's flat colours into swatch sheets; without them the board's unit look draws it plain grey. Gotcha: any sheetless unit model needs the same treatment.
