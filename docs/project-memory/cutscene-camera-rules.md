- A CUT must change the camera angle by at least 30 degrees; otherwise hold the same shot (no cut). (mondo 11-15: "another unnecesary cut, wtf. if youre gonna cut the angle needs to change by at least 30 degrees")
- Walks: the walk clip's foot speed must match how fast the body moves (no sliding); scene walks slower rather than faster.
- Turns are smooth animations, never an instant snap of heading.
- Product shots: a zoom/push on an object must centre on it exactly (the TV screen).
- When a line is said walking away, the speaker keeps talking while walking, slowly; shoot it from the listener's POV.
- Pans: close on the subject (desk), and a new subject gets a CUT, not a long pan across the room.
