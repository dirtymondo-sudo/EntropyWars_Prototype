PR #477 was merged on 2026-10-24, with sync zip r2-sync/ENTROPY_WARS_SYNC_20261024-0911.zip. It answers mondo's ask: "can enclosed areas get like a key light or something? I just feel like the game is really dark overall".

- **Cause:** a closed room's light was clamped at ambientFloor 0.55, so caves, crypts, cellars and sewers lit at hemi 0.275 and key 0.12. They read near-black (frame luminance 4-17 out of 255).
- **Key Light:** one point light with no shadows follows the camera in an enclosed room (a box room without open sky, or a bay). It is built once per visit, so the light count stays constant. In a fight in an enclosed room it hangs over the camera's focus.
- **Lift:** a dim mood is now lifted instead of clamped: floor 0.7 + 0.3 × mood. The fight's key now uses the walk's 0.34 instead of 0.22 × mood.
- **Setting:** Settings > Video > The Polish > Key Light. It is a slider (polish key `keyLight`), default 0.5, and 0 turns it off.
- **Numbers:** data.js HQ_LIGHT_RULES.keyLamp. Notes are in docs/notes/rendering-loading-perf.md under "THE KEY LIGHT", and proof shots are in /mnt/project-files/key-light/.
- **NIGHT FILL (PR #478, sync zip 20261024-1015)**, after mondo said the woods were too dark at night: at night the outdoor hemisphere now leans 50% toward a moonlight blue (`_hqSkyHemiC`), the moon key has a floor of 0.75, and the Key Light rides outdoors at 0.6 × night. Numbers are in HQ_LIGHT_RULES.nightFill. Woods night went from ~10 to ~20; the night-round fight only from 22 to 27. Day is unchanged.
- **Not changed:** the day/night clock, the looks (vignettes and tints), bloom and fog.
