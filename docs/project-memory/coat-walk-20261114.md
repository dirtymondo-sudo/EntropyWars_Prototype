Long coded coats (COAT HANG chains in three-renderer.js `hangTick`) stood out like a bell at the hip in the walk/after a turn.
Cause was NOT the walk clip: chains ran one <=1/30 s step per frame in world space, so slow frame rates and snap facing
changes left the skirt behind. Fixed PR #1400 (2026-11-14): COAT_HANG `sub` 4, `hitch` 0.25, `turnRate` 240. Notes:
docs/notes/character-creator.md "THE COAT IN THE WALK".

Still open: thin light lines at the overcoat's back waist (halfdemon M) are the tucked SHIRT, not the belt. A deeper static
tuck made it worse; cloth collision off is far worse. Classic-body saved looks show trouser specks at the seat (no fitting there by rule).

**How to apply (probing coats):** set yaw BEFORE simulating, advance past the idle->walk crossfade, step real 1/60 s frames;
a look-check on software GL runs ~2 fps and exaggerates lag. One look.js TURN unit takes ~10 min in the cloud browser.
