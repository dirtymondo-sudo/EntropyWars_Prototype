mondo 2026-11-17 (Astral Realm One World thread), picking the chain layout for the Astral Realm: "Connected/bigger rooms is better. If the room is smaller than disaster city than dont even ask, just make it one room". Disaster City has 144 plots, plus the Hollywood Hills.

**Why:** loading screens between places that belong together annoy him. The 30-plot cap in the room-splitting rule (areas-complexes.md "ONE WORLD, THE ROOM-SPLITTING RULE") is stricter than what he wants.
**How to apply:** when you split a zone or district into rooms, keep it as one room whenever its plot count and box are under Disaster City's. Don't put a rooms card in front of him. Split only where it's bigger than the city, or where a measured frame-rate or memory limit forces it, and say which. The Astral Realm also went with the chain he picked: Sea, then Library, then Nightmare, with two crossings. Related [[one-world-map-20261116]].
