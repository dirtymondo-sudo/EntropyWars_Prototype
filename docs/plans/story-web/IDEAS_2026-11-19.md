## Story arcs (the 7 cards)
1. **The Loosh Economy**: 8 units hunt loosh; make it a market. The Banker runs the exchange for the Overlord, the
   Gambler pays in loosh, Televangelist + E-Girl supply, the Popstar is product. A DOOR case: follow the money to Hell.
2. **The Stadium Strike**: Firefighter pulled the QB out; the Referee was paid (by the Gambler) to stall the game until
   the strike; Politician F runs against the President with the QB beside her; Kong fears what the Sewer Gator becomes.
3. **Hell's royal family**: the Overlord is the Prince's and Princess's father ("my father runs Hell"); heirs at war;
   the Openers + Legion alliance is the Prince's chance to prove himself on Earth.
4. **The Greys go rogue**: the Jellyfish made them as workers, they sided with the MIC; farmers, cowboys, the Scarecrow
   and the Mantids hate them. The Jellyfish UFO comes to take its workers back; the General is in the middle.
5. **Elle's marriage**: Elle Vator's husband (Tech CEO) loves the Spy, who serves the Shogun, who employs the Hacker:
   a Katana Empire honey-trap into his company, and through Elle, into DOOR.
6. **The Escapees' inside man**: the D.U.M.B. Janitor saw every lab and left the door open; the female Mad Scientist
   (ex-CERN) is the male one's rival and wants a second 1971 crossing (the Machine Elves).
7. **Still alone**: the list above.
