Entropy Wars · the world, redrawn

One World

One continent on a flat disc inside the Ice Wall, at the battle grid’s real scale: radius 44 plots = 528 battle squares, about 1.85 km; 3.7 km across. The land between the famous places is real country now: ground that changes plot by plot, farms and mills and mines that keep the cities going, and roads that run where the land lets them. The map comes in three views, so each one stays readable.

Version 13: districts as paths, and loops below

Until now every district was a solid block that I moved whole. This time I laid out the Woods, Camelot, the Estate and the Mountains one plot at a time. Each district keeps exactly the same number of plots and everything in them, but its plots now run as a path or a ring through the wild. So walking through a district is also a way across the map, beside the roads.

  • Dead Man’s Cave is the creek. It runs east from the Clearing as a dry creek bed: Culvert, Rocks, Hollow, Gully, Sewer Mouth, ending at the Storm Drain by the city. It is a second way between the Woods and Disaster City beside the Valley Road, and the tunnel way down is in its bed.

  • The Redwood Trail is the way up to the mountains. It climbs north from the Clearing through the old trees and the falls to the Snowline, and a wild link carries on to the foot of the Snowy Pass.

  • The Corn Fields are the walk to the Estate. The corn runs south from the Woods’ Fence Line to the Hub, beside County Lane, and the wells come down at the Hub.

  • The Ritual Ground is a ring round a small lake, with the Stage on its east side. Wild links join it to the Clearing, to Camelot’s Forest and down to the Haunted House.

  • The Haunted House is a drive from the Iron Gate down past the house to the graveyard and the Crypt. The Ranch runs south from its gate; the barn hatch to Research is in the Barn.

  • Camelot’s districts leave the castle in four directions. The Snowy Pines run north towards the Ski Lodge, the Looking-Glass Garden west to the coast, the Fairy Forest east to a Spring where it meets the Woods, and the Forest south towards the Ritual Ground.

  • The Mountains are climbs. Olympus runs from its Twelve Thrones down to the Pilgrim Stair. The Snowy Pass is one long climb from the Tree Line to the Ridge. The Ski Lodge is its runs, then the lodge at the bottom. Shasta climbs from its summit down switchbacks to the Upper Shelf.

The wild links join each district’s nearest plot to the next district’s nearest plot, one plot wide. They also cross between zones (the Woods to the Snowy Pass, Camelot’s Fairy Forest to the Woods, the Ritual Ground to the Haunted House, Camelot’s Snowy Pines to the Ski Lodge). Leylines’ districts are moved but not reshaped yet. Plot names inside a district are working names from what the district already is (mine).

Underground loops (mine), from the critique: the Bone Passage, a secret walk from the Catacombs to Hollow Earth, so Hell, the Catacombs and the Cavern form a loop; the Outfall, a walk from the Sewers out to the Sea Floor, joining the city’s undercity to the Deep; and the Freight Line, a late-game ride from the Radioactive Drain to Sub-Level 7, a bypass between the Sewers and the D.U.M.B. that opens after the D.U.M.B. The Holding Cells also now reach the tunnel way in the creek, so the Woods, the Sewers and the Cavern form a second loop.

Size stays frozen. No more changes to the dimensions until a test slice is walked.

How much gets built

Only the plots you can walk get built: districts, routes and zone trails. The rest of the land is scenery: what you see past a plot’s edge, drawn by the horizon builders the way Disaster City’s mountains and sea are drawn today. So the forest between the Woods’ districts is real on the map and in view, but you walk it on the trail, not across every plot of it.

How it gets built: the zones are already written by code (one bake tool per zone, from a layout file and one file per district). The routes and zone trails would work the same way. One generator dresses each trail plot from its ground kind on the Land sheet (forest, scrub, snow), and the places on the way (a well, a mill, a camp) are made by hand. The generator is my proposal; nothing like it exists yet.

Travel time: the world is about 3.7 km across, so walking from one side to the other takes roughly 15 to 25 minutes, depending on the walk speed. The map’s GO to a zone stays the way to travel far; the walk is for the stretches between near neighbours.

What changed after the critique, point by point

  1. Scale: back to the smaller world. Version 10 doubled the distances; that was too big, so version 12 returns to radius 44 plots and keeps everything else. Every place keeps its size, and every map plot is still a real 42 m plot on the battle grid, so the map is literal, not a drawing.

  2. The land changes gradually. Every land plot is one of 18 ground kinds, worked out from two numbers: its height and how wet it is. Because the same two numbers make every region, there is always a band between them: snow peaks, bare rock, alpine slopes, foothills, then the valleys; forest, open woodland, grassland, dry scrub, badlands, desert, dunes. Rivers get meadows, the Nile mouth a wetland, the Salt Lake its salt flats, and the coast has sand where it is low and cliffs where it is high.

  3. The ordinary world. 24 working places now sit between the landmarks: farms, vineyards, mills, a market town, logging and lumber, mines, a quarry and salt works, wells and a caravan stop, fishing villages, ports, a reservoir and a wind farm. They are seen from the routes, and any of them can become a stop you walk into. All are my ideas with plain names.

  4. Roads with a reason. Three kinds. Highways join the four cities (Disaster City, Vatican City, Camelot, Western Town). Roads reach towns, farms and ports and follow valleys and the coast. Trails climb to wild places and through passes. Each route says below why it runs where it does.

  5. A real coastline. Camelot’s bay is the mouth of a western river; the south coast has headlands and coves; the north has a fjord; the east coast bends round the Beach; shallows ring the land, and there is a reef chain in the south-west and skerries in the north-east.

  6. Three views instead of one. The Land shows only geography. Travel shows roads, towns and ports over a muted land (the player’s map). The Master keeps every room, route band and grid number (the builder’s map). The dotted white ring is the Round Voyage, now in the key.

  7. Why these places are together. The world is a parody of the real one, each place the ultimate form of its idea. So the map is a squashed parody of Earth’s: the Old World’s legends in the west and north (Camelot and Stonehenge for Britain, Rome and the Vatican, Olympus for Greece); America in the middle and east (Hollywood and Disaster City on the coast, the Skinwalker ranch Estate, Area 51 and Western Town in the desert, the Bermuda Triangle off the east coast); Egypt and the ancient world to the south-east; the poles at the top and bottom of a flat earth inside its Ice Wall. That reading of the layout is mine.

Sheet 4 · below

Tunnels you walk are the zone’s own plots, one plot wide. Long links are lines you ride (a train or a sub between stations), so they cost no plots and are quick to cross.

After your OK

First a test slice, as the critique suggests: the southern Woods (the Clearing and Dead Man’s Cave), County Lane and the Estate’s Lake, with their zone trails. That shows whether the walking, the fights on trail plots and the spacing feel right before anything else moves. Then, smallest first, each its own PR: (1) the new places in data.js HQ_ZONE_MAP and the map sheets, (2) the zone edges and horizons, so each room looks out on the right ground kind and its true neighbours, (3) the route kit with its three looks (highway, road, trail), then R1, R6 and R8, (4) the other routes and the Desert Highway, (5) the Volcano Island’s move and growth, the Triangle’s move and the Round Voyage, (6) the new islands, (7) the Colosseum, the Maglev and the Internet, (8) the working places, as scenery first.

Totals: 247 route plots on the surface, 51 zone trail plots, 98 walked tunnel plots underground (the Maglev and the Freight Line are rides), 60 new island plots, 9 for the Colosseum and 36 for the Internet. That is 501 new plots. Nothing is deleted. The big version had 1,377; the first version, 414.
