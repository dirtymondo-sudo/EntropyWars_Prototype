# THE VATICAN: THE DRESSING PLAN (thread 11; a proposal, 2026-11-20)

The Vatican is NOT a height job. Its ground is right (13 m, the Observatory's mount, the Colosseum, the Gardens). It is a DRESSING job:
the first build stands and most of what makes each place read is missing. This list is drawn from each district file's own "NOT YET"
lines, the board's rows and the screenshot audit. It moves no ground and lays no new way, so there is no `draw-plan.js` file for it.

**Who has what:** the Basilica (B) is the second account's pilot (4 passes live): NOT in this plan. The Colosseum (O) is done. This plan
is the Square (S), the Cloister (C), the Palace (P) and what is left of the Gardens (G).

**Before placing anything (owner-rules.md, THE MODEL LIST FIRST):** the build thread reads `MODEL_LIST.md` and the pack files in full for
church, marble, statue, garden, fountain, market, pilgrim, banner, lamp. The kinds below say WHAT stands where; the thread picks the piece.
Where the game has no piece, it asks mondo for a model: it never draws a stand-in.

## The order (pilot first)

1. **THE CLOISTER (C, 3 plots): the pilot.** Smallest, and the audit names it. Then mondo looks.
2. **THE SQUARE (S, 8 plots).**
3. **THE PALACE (P, 3 plots).**
4. **THE GARDENS (G, 4 plots): what is left.**

## C · THE CLOISTER (the Cloister, the Cistern, the Fountain)

Stands: the arcade's columns, four pines, the well, the fountain, benches, the gardener.

| Add | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The arcade's ROOF (a lean-to of tile on the columns) and its BACK WALL with doors and small windows | all four sides of the garth | today the columns stand in the open: it does not read as a cloister |
| The cross of walks in the garth, paved; four flower beds in its quarters; a sundial or a small font where the walks meet | the garth | an open, level place on purpose |
| The lanes' walls and two iron gates | between the Cloister, the Cistern and the Fountain | so the three courts are three places, with a way between each |
| THE GARDENER'S SHED: a real walk-in lean-to (tools, pots, seed trays, a cot) with a working door | the Fountain's court, against the south wall | the district's building ("a gardener's door"); owner-rules: a secret is a mundane door |
| The Cistern's ring: marble kerb, the rope and its winch, a bucket, wet stone | the Cistern | the well down to the Catacombs should be found by its rope |
| Light: lanterns under the arcade, one lamp at the well | the arcade, the Cistern | reads at night |

## S · THE SQUARE (the oval, the colonnades, the Gate)

Stands: 60 columns in two arcs, the obelisk, two fountains, the gate (open since One World), lamps, benches, four people.

| Add | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The colonnade's ROOF (an entablature on the columns) and the FIGURES on it (statues along both arcs) | the two arcs | the Square's silhouette: the landmark seen from the River Road |
| PAVING BANDS: the oval's spokes from the obelisk, a ring round each fountain | the oval | orientation: the spokes point at the obelisk from every side |
| THE GATE'S HOUSE: a real walk-in gatehouse (a guard room, a stair to the wall walk) | the Gate plot, in the east wall | the district's building; the way out to the River Road gets a front |
| The four corners: a pilgrims' stall row, a fountain basin with steps, a row of flags, a bell on a frame | one in each corner plot | the corners are empty today |
| Pilgrims' barriers in lines toward the Basilica's doors; more people (a big outdoor place wants more bodies) | the west side of the oval | it leads the eye to the Basilica |

Kept level on purpose: the whole oval (a square: mondo 11-20, "there can still be open areas, still be flat areas").

## P · THE PALACE (the Observatory, the Archive, the Courtyard)

Stands: the mount and its rails, columns and braziers on top; the Archive's hall with its stacks; the Courtyard's arcade.

| Add | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The Observatory's DOME (open slit, the telescope under it) | the top tier of the mount | the Palace's landmark; the way up to Heaven starts under it |
| The Archive's GALLERY inside (a stair, a walk round the stacks), reading tables, windows | the Archive | its inside is one bare hall today |
| A PALACE FRONT: a real facade on the Archive's south side (a door case, windows in rows, a cornice) | the lane before the Archive | the outside does not say "palace" |
| The Terrace's balustrade and statues; planters | the Courtyard's north side | the Terrace is a bare step today |

**Asked of mondo:** the audit found the palace's domes standing too close together and wearing one blurry stretched picture (BIG WALLS ARE
BLURRY UP CLOSE, section F). Fixing the picture is the open question 4 of the plan (its own thread, or with each zone).

## G · THE GARDENS (what is left)

Stands: the Hedge Maze, the Campanile's stump, pines, statues. Add: flower beds and topiary along the walks, the wall walk on the garden
wall (a found way up: a view over the Tiber), the Campanile's bell.

## THE CHECK for this job (section A's check, the lines that apply)

- Does each district have one building you walk into (the gardener's shed, the gatehouse, the Archive) and does its inside match its outside?
- From each way into the room, do you see a landmark (the obelisk, the Observatory's dome, the Basilica's dome)?
- Is every open place still open (the oval, the garth, the Courtyard)? Nothing blocks a walk; props sit on whole squares.
