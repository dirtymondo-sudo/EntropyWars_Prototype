   in the door").

   the cover-up is the crime.

4. One straight hallway/section under HQ (H-Wing), found late game.
5. Ending seed: the player becomes the Doorman; the Doorman was the
   janitor all along.

## 4. Scene scripts (one chapter at a time)

Write chapters 1–3 fully and hand them over while writing 4+ — the
milestone delivery model makes the story modular, and pacing is best
judged in-game. Format (maps 1:1 onto `playCutscene`; stage directions
sparingly, in brackets — the engine's vocabulary is portraits, dialogue,
camera, SFX, not blocking):

```
### C1-S1 · "<scene title>" (~60s, normal | important | reveal)
LOCATION: <room / context — e.g. Your office · after the first mission>
SPEAKERS: HANDLER (race), PLAYER
[location_card]
HANDLER: line
PLAYER: line
[sfx: doorbell]
HANDLER: line
```

Scene discipline: open with a one-line re-anchor (the player may be ten
matches past the last scene) · one job per scene · 30–90 s = 6–15 lines ·
3–6 minute scenes only for the opening, major reveals, chapter finales
and the ending.
